# ADR-006 — Practice companion, not a tutor

**Status:** Accepted · 7 Nov 2026 · **Phase:** P0 (governs all copy and UI)

## Context
"Learn ASL in VR" is a claim we cannot earn in 11 days, and one the literature does not support (a 60-participant RCT of a comparable VR system found no significant platform effect on immediate recall). Overclaiming is also the specific thing that would make a Deaf judge distrust the product.

## Options
1. **Tutor** — bigger promise, larger perceived market, indefensible in the time available and in front of the literature.
2. **Practice companion** — narrower, defensible, and it makes the honesty (blind spots, anti-claim) coherent rather than defensive.

## Decision
**Handshape practice companion.** Stated in the app, in the description, and in the credits screen: *"This is practice software, not a replacement for a Deaf teacher."*

## Consequences
- Every piece of copy is constrained by this. No "become fluent," no "master ASL."
- The anti-claim paragraph becomes a strength of the submission instead of an apology.
- The product's promise shrinks to something testable: *inspect a 3D handshape from any angle; be told which sign you actually produced.* Both are demonstrable in 60 seconds.

## Revisit if
We partner with a Deaf-led curriculum provider — at which point the claim can grow with the evidence.
