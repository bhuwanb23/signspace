# P8.1 — Recruit three testers with different hands

**Phase:** P8 · **Est:** 45 min · **Depends on:** P7 · **Workstream:** W1
**Gate fed:** G8

## Why this exists
Three testers with different hand sizes catch most of what a judge will experience. One with limited dexterity catches things no other tester can.

## Do this
1. Recruit: one small-handed, one large-handed, one with limited dexterity or a hand difference if findable.
2. None of them may have seen the app or read anything about it.
3. Brief them with exactly one sentence: *'Put this on and see what you think.'* Nothing else.
4. Get consent to record hands, audio and the passthrough view.

## Done when
- [ ] Three testers confirmed, none briefed
- [ ] Recording consent obtained

## Artefacts produced
- `phases/p8-playtest/testers.md` (first names or initials only)

## Traps
- Testing on teammates — they know what it's supposed to do and will unconsciously complete it
