# P8.4 — Fix the top three, and only the top three

**Phase:** P8 · **Est:** 2 h · **Depends on:** P8.3 · **Workstream:** W1
**Gate fed:** G8

## Why this exists
Fixing three things properly beats fixing ten things partially, and Day 10 is a submission day, not a build day.

## Do this
1. Fix the top three friction points, smallest viable change each.
2. No refactors. No new systems. No new assets unless the friction is an asset problem.
3. Re-run the exact scenario that produced each friction point.
4. If a fix introduces a new problem, revert it — a known friction is better than an unknown defect.

## Done when
- [ ] Three fixes shipped and verified
- [ ] No new defect introduced
- [ ] Nothing else touched

## Artefacts produced
- `phases/p8-playtest/fixes.md`

## Traps
- Fixing five because they were quick — that's how a build breaks on Day 10
