# P8 — Playtest & iterate

**Day 9 (Sun 15 Nov) · Budget 6 h · Gate:** G8 — Does a stranger reach their first mastered shape in under 75 seconds, with nobody helping?
**Status:** ⬜ Not started

## Objective
Watch three strangers use it, write down everything that goes wrong, and fix only the three worst things.

## Why it sits here
Every assumption about how the first five minutes land is untested until someone who isn't us touches it. One day of watching strangers is worth more than any further internal polish.

## Inputs required before starting
- A polished build (P7 green)
- Three uncoached testers
- Recording setup

## Outputs — the phase is done when all of these are true
- [ ] A friction log with real behavioural evidence
- [ ] Three fixes
- [ ] A measured time-to-first-lock
