5b. WRIST-ONLY NOISE - O for a correct hand, 2 deg on the wrist and palm (2000 trials)
   O: mean 2.42  sd 1.41  p95 5.07  p99 6.43  max 9.06

==============================================================================
DECISION: O_slack = 15.0 deg, k = 1.00
==============================================================================
  the slack is the task's own figure and survives the measurement: inside 15 deg the term
  contributes exactly zero, so a naturally different resting wrist is not punished (ADR-003).
  k is the smallest weight on the tested list that puts a 30 deg mis-face at least 3 deg past
  the lock band - a gate that only pushes a wrong hand just over the line is a coin flip.
