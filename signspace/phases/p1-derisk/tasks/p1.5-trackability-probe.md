**One bug found before it could produce a false result:** the first pass at the twenty references left
**M and N 10.3° apart** — inside the 12° lock threshold — so the probe would have reported a
"tracks as a different shape" failure caused entirely by the reference poses. N was re-authored (ring
and little loosely curled rather than stacked) and the pair moved to 29.5° / 70.1°. Every pair of the
twenty is now checked against the metric before device work, and the two pairs that remain inside the
close window (U↔R 14.6°, L↔G 21.6°) are flagged in advance as expected confusion.

## Why this exists
The whole content scope depends on what the sensor can actually see. Guessing here is how projects ship a shape that never locks — the worst possible demo failure.

## Do this
1. Pick 20 candidate ASL handshapes: the 12 from `SIGNSET.md`, the 5 suspected blind spots (M, N, T, R, S), plus E, X and one wildcard.
2. In a daylight room, hold each shape for 10 s and record: does it track? does it flicker? does it track *as a different shape*?
3. Repeat in lamplight only.
4. Repeat both lighting conditions with your non-dominant hand.
5. Score each shape: High / Medium / Low / Never, and note the failure mode.
6. Record the results directly into `docs/03-content/TRACKABILITY.md` §2.

## Done when
- [ ] 20 shapes × 2 lighting conditions tested — ⬜ **device**; rig and references are ready
