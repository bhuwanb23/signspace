# P1.3 — Rotate the grabbed object with your own wrist

**Phase:** P1 · **Est:** 1.5 h · **Depends on:** P1.2 · **Workstream:** W2
**Gate fed:** G1

## Why this exists
**This is the make-or-break test of the whole concept.** If grabbing a floating hand and turning it with your wrist feels wrong, the product's core gesture is wrong and we re-plan on Day 2 rather than Day 8.

## Do this
1. Make the grabbed object a proxy for a hand (a simple hand block or the P1.1 mesh).
2. Attach rotation to wrist quaternion delta, with position held stable near the grab point.
3. Test three variants and rate each 1–5: (a) direct 1:1 wrist coupling, (b) damped 1:1 with slight lag, (c) hold-still-to-rotate with the hand held in place.
4. Try all three at arm's length (~0.4 m) and at lap distance (~0.6 m) — the two distances the product will use.
5. Write a verdict: which variant, and whether the gesture is good enough to build a product on.

## Done when
- [ ] A chosen rotation variant that feels natural at both distances
- [ ] A written go/no-go with a reason
- [ ] Position drift under rotation measured (should be near zero)

## Artefacts produced
- `phases/p1-derisk/rotation-verdict.md` — the most important page produced on Day 2

## Traps
- Testing only with a cube — a hand has a familiar shape and your brain rejects wrong rotation far more harshly
- Following through to the next task without writing the verdict down
