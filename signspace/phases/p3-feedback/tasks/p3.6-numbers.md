# P3.6 — Verify the honest-mirror numbers on device

**Phase:** P3 · **Est:** 30 min · **Depends on:** P3.4 · **Workstream:** W3
**Gate fed:** G3

## Why this exists
The panel's credibility rests on its numbers being true. An invented count is the one thing that would turn the honest mirror into a marketing claim.

## Do this
1. Count the shape variants actually tested in P1.5 and the ones rated High/Medium.
2. Re-test the borderline shapes (S, E) three times each in lamplight.
3. Write the final count into the panel copy and into `TRACKABILITY.md` §4.
4. Add a date to the panel: *'measured on Quest 3S, <date>'* — limits change with SDK versions.

## Run sheet — prepared, not run (30 min on a headset)

So the half hour is mechanical when the headset is available, and so nothing is invented in the meantime.
The file to edit is `unity/Assets/StreamingAssets/trackability.json`; **every number lands in that one
file** and the panel, the count line and the doc all read from it.

| # | Step | Where the result goes | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `ProbeController`, 10 s hold per shape, **normal light**, all 20 | `status` → `tracks-and-locks` / `not-resolvable`, `lockRate` per row | 12 min |
| 2 | **S and E**, three holds each, **lamplight** (the two borderline shapes the task names) | their rows' `lockRate` becomes the mean of three; if they disagree, `note` says so and the row stays `may-resolve` | 8 min |
| 3 | **F** once, to confirm the re-authored contact survives | `status` | 3 min |
| 4 | The six `fine` rows once more, eyes only: does each still lock? | a row that fails is **demoted**, not excused | 4 min |
| 5 | Flip `measured` to `true`, set `measuredUtc` to today, `device`, `conditions` | the panel's count line becomes the dated form by itself — no code change | 1 min |
| 6 | Read the panel on device and compare to `TRACKABILITY.md` §3 | mismatch = fix the file, not the doc | 2 min |

**Do not** touch `expected` during this. `expected` is the design decision; `status`/`lockRate` are what
the device said. The moment they get edited together, the document stops being able to show what changed.

## The two scripts that make the half hour mechanical

| Script | What it does | Why it is not an editor note |
|---|---|---|
| `scripts/fill_trackability.py` | reads `probe-*.jsonl` and writes `trackability.json`: `status`, `lockRate`, `rates`, `borderline`, a measured `note`, and — only when all twenty shapes have a result — `measured:true`, the date, the device and the conditions. Also regenerates §3.1/§3.2/§4.2 of the doc. | **`expected` is compared before and after and the script refuses to write if it moved.** The design decision and the measurement cannot be edited together |
| `scripts/check_trackability.py` | the gate: 20 variants, ids that have pose files, valid words, and then the state rule — *unmeasured means every row unmeasured and every rate −1; measured means every row measured, a real ISO date, a device and conditions* — plus borderline rows that must have repeated holds behind them, plus the panel's sentence appearing **verbatim** in the doc | an invented number now fails a command instead of surviving a review |

Dry run, no headset required:

```
python3 scripts/fill_trackability.py <logs> --dry-run     # prints the exact sentence the panel will show
python3 scripts/check_trackability.py                     # fails on a half-filled or drifted file
```

Both were exercised end to end on **synthetic** logs in `/tmp` (never written into the repo): a
full twenty with four failures produced `14 to 15 of 20 …` — the range coming from S, whose three
lamplight holds came out 0.55 / 0.78 / 0.62 — and four deliberately broken files (half-filled, no date,
a `borderline` flag with one hold behind it, a doc that had drifted) each failed with the violation named.

## Done when
- [x] **The count in the panel matches the count in the doc** — enforced, not checked by eye:
      `check_trackability.py` fails unless the panel's exact sentence appears in the doc, and the doc's
      tables are generated from the file (`--emit-doc`)
- [x] **Borderline shapes resolved or the count phrased as a range** — `borderline` means the holds
      straddle the floor; the count becomes `N to N+k` and the panel adds *"Repeated holds disagreed on
      two; the range is those shapes."* A borderline shape is excluded from the lower bound only
- [x] **Measurement date shown** — the dated form is unconditional in `Trackability.CountLine`, and the
      gate rejects `measured:true` with no ISO date
- [ ] **The measurement itself** — the only open item, and the reason the panel currently prints four
      rather than a fraction

## Blocked on
A Quest 3S, a normal room, and the two lighting conditions — then 30 minutes with the run sheet above.
Everything up to the measurement is built; the measurement is the only thing left, which is why the
number is currently absent rather than estimated.

## Traps
- Rounding the count upward to look better — the precision is the feature
