# P3.6 — Verify the honest-mirror numbers on device

**Phase:** P3 · **Est:** 30 min · **Depends on:** P3.4 · **Workstream:** W3
**Gate fed:** G3

## Why this exists
The panel's credibility rests on its numbers being true. An invented count is the one thing that would turn the honest mirror into a marketing claim.

## Do this
1. Count the shape variants actually tested in P1.5 and the ones rated High/Medium.
2. Re-test the borderline shapes (S, E) three times each in lamplight.
3. Write the final count into the panel copy and into `TRACKABILITY.md` §4.
4. Add a date to the panel: *'measured on Quest 3S, <date>'* — limits change with SDK versions.

## Done when
- [ ] The count in the panel matches the count in the doc
- [ ] Borderline shapes resolved or the count phrased as a range
- [ ] Measurement date shown

## Artefacts produced
- `docs/03-content/TRACKABILITY.md` §4 final

## Traps
- Rounding the count upward to look better — the precision is the feature
