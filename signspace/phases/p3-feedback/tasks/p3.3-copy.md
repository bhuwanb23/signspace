# P3.3 — Write the near-miss copy

**Phase:** P3 · **Est:** 45 min · **Depends on:** P3.1 · **Workstream:** W4
**Gate fed:** G3

## Why this exists
The line appears at the emotional low point of a learner's attempt. It must be warm, short, and specific — a scolding or a paragraph will undo the whole mechanic.

## Do this
1. Draft 10 variants of the line. Constraints: ≤12 words, no 'incorrect', no 'error', no exclamation marks.
2. Test each on a non-signing friend and ask what they think the app wants them to do next.
3. Pick the clearest; keep a second for the accessibility (high-contrast, text-only) path.
4. Record the final string in `docs/06-submission/SUBMISSION-COPY.md` under 'in-product copy'.

## Done when
- [x] A final string ≤12 words — **"That's O — 3° away. C opens a little."** = 9 words (worst case 11,
      plain form 6)
- [~] Understood by a non-signer in one read — one reader, and that reader is on the project. Pick
      recorded; the two-reader test in `copy-variants.md` §5 is still owed and the criterion stays open
- [x] No negative framing — the old "That's not C" is replaced; lines assert what the hand is and what the
      target needs
