# P7.1 — Sound design: every state change has a sound

**Phase:** P7 · **Est:** 2 h · **Depends on:** P3.5 · **Workstream:** W1
**Gate fed:** G7

## Why this exists
Sound is a scored sub-criterion and the cheapest emotional upgrade in the build. It is also how we replace text without adding words.

## Do this
1. Build the event→sound map from `SOUND-MAP.md` and fill any gaps found.
2. Design the signature lock chime — it plays hundreds of times, so it must be short, warm, and non-fatiguing.
3. Give the near-miss a distinct, gentler sound — informational, not corrective.
4. Spatialise the shelf and reference-hand sounds; keep UI sounds head-locked.
5. Mix: nothing above −6 dBFS peak, no music bed, silence is a deliberate choice in three states.

## Done when
- [ ] Every state in `UX-STATES.md` has a sound or a documented silence
- [ ] Lock chime tested for fatigue over 50 plays
- [ ] Mix levels recorded

## Artefacts produced
- `assets/audio/` populated
- `docs/04-design/SOUND-MAP.md` (complete)

## Traps
- Adding ambient music — it dates the product and competes with the chime
