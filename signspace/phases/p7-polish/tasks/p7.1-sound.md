# P7.1 — Sound design: every state change has a sound

**Phase:** P7 · **Est:** 2 h · **Depends on:** P3.5 · **Workstream:** W1
**Gate fed:** G7

## Why this exists
Sound is a scored sub-criterion and the cheapest emotional upgrade in the build. It is also how we replace text without adding words.

## Do this
1. Build the event→sound map from `SOUND-MAP.md` and fill any gaps found.
2. Design the signature lock chime — it plays hundreds of times, so it must be short, warm, and non-fatiguing.
3. Give the near-miss a distinct, gentler sound — informational, not corrective.
4. Spatialise the shelf and reference-hand sounds; keep UI sounds head-locked.
5. Mix: nothing above −6 dBFS peak, no music bed, silence is a deliberate choice in three states.

## Done when
- [x] Every state in `UX-STATES.md` has a sound or a documented silence — **desk-verified**
- [ ] Lock chime tested for fatigue over 50 plays — **NOT RUN** (ears, `HUMAN-TASKS` H38)
- [x] Mix levels recorded — every clip's peak measured from the imported WAV, all at or under −6 dBFS

## Artefacts produced
- `assets/audio/` populated
- `docs/04-design/SOUND-MAP.md` (complete)

## Traps
- Adding ambient music — it dates the product and competes with the chime

## Build status (17 Nov 2026)

**Shipped.** `scripts/make_sfx.py` generates sixteen mono WAVs (596 kB total) from this map's
descriptions; `SoundDirector` plays twelve cues from three pooled sources (body, room, rotation loop);
`BandAudio` keeps the three band sounds. `P7.1 Check sound` asserts every cue has a clip, every peak is
at or under −6 dBFS, the chime is 1.2–2.0 s, the near-miss is quieter and shorter, and the two are not
the same interval (spectral-ratio comparison).

**Two real defects this found.** The shipped chime peaked at **−4.1 dBFS**, over the map's own ceiling;
and ten of the map's states had no clip at all. The chime also had no reverb tail and no wooden
partials — it was a plain bell-ish tone. All three are fixed.

**Cut, on purpose.** The rotation texture is a speed-driven loop, but the granularity the map imagines
is a placeholder: a filtered noise bed with amplitude grit, not a granular synth.

**NOT RUN:** the fatigue judgement (50 plays, by ear) and the real recorded wood. The synth can prove
shape, level and duration; it cannot prove timbre or pleasantness.
