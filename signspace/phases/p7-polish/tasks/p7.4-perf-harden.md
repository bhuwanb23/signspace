# P7.4 — Performance hardening on Quest 3S

**Phase:** P7 · **Est:** 1.5 h · **Depends on:** P1.8 · **Workstream:** W2
**Gate fed:** G7

## Why this exists
60 fps is the rubric floor; 72 is ours. This is where the budget in the tech-stack document becomes a measurement with a recording attached.

## Do this
1. Enable fixed foveated rendering (medium); test MSAA 4× vs 2× and keep what holds 72.
2. Audit materials and textures: no realtime shadows, zero realtime lights, textures ≤512 except one hero.
3. Run a 15-minute thermal test with both hands tracked, 12 shelf objects present, and passthrough on.
4. Record the OVR Metrics overlay for the whole run.
5. Fill the measured table in `docs/02-tech/PERFORMANCE.md` §2.

## Done when
- [ ] ≥72 fps sustained for 15 min on Quest 3S
- [ ] Thermal curve recorded
- [ ] Overlay recording captured for the video

## Artefacts produced
- `docs/02-tech/PERFORMANCE.md` (measured)
- `assets/raw/video/perf-overlay.mp4`

- Measuring the first 60 seconds — thermal throttling is a minutes-scale phenomenon
