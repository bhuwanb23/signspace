# P0.8 — Sign off the risk register and the Definition of Done

**Phase:** P0 · **Est:** 30 min · **Depends on:** nothing · **Workstream:** W5
**Gate fed:** G0

## Why this exists
The risks and the done-criteria already exist as documents. Signing them off turns them from prose into commitments, and gives every later gate something objective to test against.

## Do this
1. Read `docs/05-build/RISKS.md` and confirm each risk has an owner and a trigger date.
2. Read `docs/05-build/DEFINITION-OF-DONE.md` and fill in any `[TBD]` in the per-phase criteria.
3. Confirm the cut ladder order in `MASTER-PLAN.md` §6 is still the order you'd actually cut in.
4. Record the sign-off date at the bottom of both documents.

## Done when
- [ ] Both documents signed and dated
- [ ] Cut ladder explicitly agreed
- [ ] No risk without an owner

## Artefacts produced
- Sign-off line in `RISKS.md` and `DEFINITION-OF-DONE.md`

## Traps
- Treating the risk register as decoration — it is read at every gate
