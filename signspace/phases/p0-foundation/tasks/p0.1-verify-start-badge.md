## Why this exists
The competition is only open to Start program members. If the badge isn't active on the submitting account, nothing else in this repo matters — and it is the kind of thing that takes a week to fix if you discover it on Day 10.

## Do this
1. Log in to the Meta developer dashboard with the account that will submit.
2. Confirm **Developer Access is enabled** — membership is useless without it (Rules §2(c)).
3. Confirm **Start membership is active**, using the strongest evidence available (status page → acceptance email → entry form).
4. Archive the screenshot as `phases/p0-foundation/evidence/p0.1-start-status-<YYYY-MM-DD>.png`, redacted.
5. Confirm the Devpost account email matches, or is deliberately linked to, that developer account.
6. Fill in **`phases/p0-foundation/accounts.md`** §3 (self-check), §4 (evidence) and §5 (linkage).
7. If membership is missing or pending: run the decision tree in `accounts.md` §6 **today** and start its escalation clock.

## Traps
- Assuming Devpost and Meta accounts are linked when they aren't — *the linkage trap, `accounts.md` §5*
- Using a personal account that isn't the program member
- Confusing **Developer Access** with **Start membership**: they are two different switches and either can be off
- Treating "allowed to join until submission day" as "safe to leave until submission day" — no published approval SLA
- Recording membership from a *teammate's* account when a different account will submit
