# P0.9 — Open the signer pipeline today (kill-condition lead time)

**Phase:** P0 · **Est:** 60 min · **Depends on:** nothing · **Workstream:** W3
**Gate fed:** G0

## Why this exists
**This is the only task in P0 that can kill the project.** Capturing named Deaf signers is the mitigation for the one fatal risk, it depends on another person's calendar, and its lead time is longer than any technical task.

## Do this
1. Write the one-page brief for a prospective signer: what we're building, what we'd ask them to do, time commitment, compensation, and how they'd be credited.
2. Draft the consent and rights scope: competition submission, video, and future commercial release.
3. Identify three routes to a signer: local Deaf association, ASL tutor networks, Deaf-led creator communities.
4. Send the brief to at least three contacts today.
5. Put the booking target in the calendar: **session must happen by Day 5 (Wed 11 Nov)**.
6. If no route responds by the end of Day 2, escalate to the fallback-concepts decision (see `RISKS.md` R1).

## Done when
- [ ] Brief written and sent to ≥3 contacts
- [ ] Consent draft ready to sign
- [ ] Calendar holds the Day-5 capture slot
- [ ] Escalation date set

## Artefacts produced
- `docs/03-content/CAPTURE-PROTOCOL.md` §1–2 (brief + consent)
- `phases/p0-foundation/signer-pipeline.md` with contact log

## Traps
- Treating this as a P5 task because the master plan files it there — the *lead time* is P0
- Offering exposure instead of payment
- Waiting for a perfect consent template instead of sending the brief
