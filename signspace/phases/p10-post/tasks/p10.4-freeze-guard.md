# P10.4 — Guard the freeze

**Phase:** P10 · **Est:** 5 min/week · **Depends on:** P9.8 · **Workstream:** W5
**Gate fed:** G10

## Why this exists
Three weeks is long enough for an idle 'quick fix' to break the submitted build. The freeze is a commitment.

## Do this
1. No commits to `main` until judging closes.
2. Any necessary fix goes to a branch and is only merged if the channel is actually broken.
3. Re-verify P10.1 weekly on the same day.

## Done when
- [ ] Branch untouched — **main has 4 post-freeze commits** (paperwork; artifact unchanged)
- [ ] Weekly verification logged — 0 of 3 rows

## Artefacts produced
- `phases/p10-post/reverify-log.md` continued

## Traps
- Refactoring in the quiet weeks

## Outcome — 18 Nov 2026

The guard is `scripts/freeze_check.py`, designed to be re-run weekly on the same weekday and appended to
`reverify-log.md` without editing earlier rows. It is deliberately hostile to optimism: a missing tag is a
FAIL, an empty commit field is a FAIL, and the three account-side questions are NOT RUN rather than
assumed.

**The guard's first act was to fail its own project.** Three commits landed after the frozen-at timestamp
and there is no tag, so criterion 3 of G10 is red before any judge looks. The APK is untouched — hash and
mtime both unchanged — which is the distinction the log draws between *the letter* and *the bytes*.
