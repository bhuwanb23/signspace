## Outcome — 18 Nov 2026

`POSTMORTEM.md` states its first finding before its first table: **nobody timed the work**, so hours are
NOT RECORDED and G10's corresponding criterion cannot pass. In their place it uses measured data — 4
calendar days, 57 commits, 723 tracked files, 116,280 insertions, and 16.8 h of commit windows as an
explicit lower bound — and separates measured from derived in every row.

It names **G8** as the gate closest to red, because the phase built the instrument and never ran the
people, and it draws three carry-forward lessons: assert the user-visible path rather than the component;
a freeze without a tag and a commit hash protects nothing; desk verification quietly replaces the thing it
stands in for.
