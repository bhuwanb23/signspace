# P10.1 — Morning re-verification

**Phase:** P10 · **Est:** 15 min · **Depends on:** P9.8 · **Workstream:** W5
**Gate fed:** G10

## Why this exists
The single most avoidable way to lose: the entry is fine on Tuesday night and broken on Wednesday morning.

## Do this
1. Open the invite URL from a clean account.
2. Confirm the `Competition` channel still lists the frozen version.
3. Confirm the video still plays publicly.
4. Confirm the description and tagline are unchanged.
5. Log the check with a timestamp.

## Done when
- [ ] All four checks pass
- [ ] Logged

## Artefacts produced
- `phases/p10-post/reverify-log.md`

## Traps
- Assuming nothing can change overnight — channel reviews can reject a build
