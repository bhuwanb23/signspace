## Why this exists
Submit on Day 11, not Day 12. The buffer day exists for verification, not for building.

## Do this
1. Open the Devpost draft from P0.2 and fill every field from the repository, not from memory.
2. Upload the video, the screenshots, the thumbnail, the invite URL and the APK reference.
3. Re-read the rules page for any change since P0.2 and confirm compliance.
4. Click submit, then archive the confirmation email and a screenshot of the submitted state.
5. Record the submission ID and timestamp.
