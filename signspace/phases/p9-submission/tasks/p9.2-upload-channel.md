# P9.2 — Upload to the `Competition` release channel

**Phase:** P9 · **Est:** 1 h · **Depends on:** P9.1 · **Workstream:** W5
**Gate fed:** G9

## Why this exists
The submission is judged on what is on that channel, not on what is on your laptop.

## Do this
1. Upload the frozen APK to the `Competition` channel with a complete store listing minimum (icon, short description, screenshots placeholder).
2. Fill every mandatory metadata field required for the channel to publish.
3. Submit for review if the channel requires it; note the expected review time and whether the competition accepts builds in review.
4. Verify the version string visible to an installer matches `1.0.0-competition`.
5. Screenshot the channel state.

## Done when
- [ ] Frozen APK live on `Competition`
- [ ] Version string verified
- [ ] Review state known and recorded

## Artefacts produced
- `phases/p9-submission/channel-state.png`

## Traps
- Discovering a mandatory field on the last day — this is why P0.3 created the channel early
