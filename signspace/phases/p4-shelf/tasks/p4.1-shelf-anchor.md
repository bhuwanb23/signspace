# P4.1 — Anchor the shelf to a real surface

**Phase:** P4 · **Est:** 2 h · **Depends on:** P1.6 · **Workstream:** W2
**Gate fed:** G4

## Why this exists
The product's promise is that your room accumulates your progress. That promise is one anchor and one layout rule.

## Do this
1. Query MRUK for horizontal surfaces within a 0.3–1.0 m band and 60° of forward.
2. Score candidates by: proximity to the user, height (waist-level preferred), and free area for a 0.45 m shelf.
3. Place the shelf at the chosen anchor, rotated to face the user, with a 2 cm clearance above the surface.
4. If no surface qualifies, go straight to the plinth path (P4.4) — never block.
5. Log which condition was chosen for every launch (useful for P8 and for the video).

## Done when
- [ ] Shelf lands on a real surface and faces the user
- [ ] Selection logic logged
- [ ] No-surface case routes to the plinth without delay
