# P4.2 — Mastered shapes become shelf objects

**Phase:** P4 · **Est:** 2 h · **Depends on:** P4.1 · **Workstream:** W1
**Gate fed:** G4

## Why this exists
The shelf is a progress bar you can touch. If the objects don't read as *the shape you learned*, it's just a counter.

## Do this
1. Design one placeholder object per shape family (12 distinct silhouettes are not needed on Day 5).
2. On lock (P2.5), spawn the object at the hand and fly it to its shelf slot on a 500 ms arc.
3. Give each object a resting state: a slow, subtle idle (breathing) that reads as 'alive' but costs no CPU.
4. Add the empty-slot affordance: the next slot is visible and dimmed from the first launch.
5. Confirm 12 objects + 12 slots stays inside the draw-call and triangle budget.

## Done when
- [ ] Objects spawn and settle without overlap
- [ ] Empty slot visible from launch
- [ ] Budget still met with 12 objects present

## Artefacts produced
- `unity/Assets/_Project/Prefabs/ShelfObject.prefab`

## Traps
- Animating 12 objects continuously — idle at <1% CPU or drop the idle
