# P4.2 — Mastered shapes become shelf objects

**Phase:** P4 · **Est:** 2 h · **Depends on:** P4.1 · **Workstream:** W1
**Gate fed:** G4

## Why this exists
The shelf is a progress bar you can touch. If the objects don't read as *the shape you learned*, it's just a counter.

## Do this
1. [x] Design one placeholder object per shape family (12 distinct silhouettes are not needed on Day 5).
   → **4 families, 6 shapes**: fist B · pair U/V · triple W · curve C/O. One cached mesh per shape,
   built from boxes at runtime (`Spatial/ShelfSilhouettes.cs`).
2. [x] On lock (P2.5), spawn the object at the hand and fly it to its shelf slot on a 500 ms arc.
   → `LockPayoff` asks `ShelfAnchor.TryGetSlotFor(shapeId)` and arcs to that slot; `ShelfObjectView`
   gives the object its shape. 12 slots, 6 × 2 on the plate.
3. [x] Give each object a resting state: a slow, subtle idle (breathing) that reads as 'alive' but
   has minimal CPU cost. → `Spatial/ShelfIdle.cs`, **one** Update for all objects, 0.18 Hz, ±0.6%, switch
   `breathing` on the component.
4. [x] Add the empty-slot affordance: the next slot is visible and dimmed from the first launch.
   → `ShelfAnchor.RefreshSlots()`: one dimmed marker at the next empty slot, visible from launch 1,
   dimmed through `slotEmptyMaterial` or a `MaterialPropertyBlock` if nobody wired one.
5. [x] Confirm 12 objects + 12 slots stays inside the draw-call and triangle budget.
   → `scripts/shelf_budget.py` → `phases/p4-shelf/budget-check.md`: **26 / 120 draw calls,
   912 / 150k triangles, 4 / 12 materials**. The script reads the recipes and the ceilings out of
   the repo and exits 1 on a breach; `--slots 200` is a negative control. This is a shelf-only,
   single-pass estimate for a hypothetical 12-object load, not a whole-frame measurement.
   **Verified against built objects (16 Nov):** `SIGNSPACE/P4.2 Shelf check` instantiates twelve
   shipped prefabs in twelve shipped slots and counts **26 draw calls / 864 worst-case object
triangles**; the estimate's 912 adds 24 marker triangles and 24 for the plate and plinth, so the
   recipe count and the rendered meshes agree.

## Done when
- [x] Objects spawn and settle without overlap — **marked from measurement, not from code**:
  `ShelfCheck` fills all twelve slots with the shipped `ShelfObject.prefab` and the shipped meshes,
  then tests every pair of world bounds: **0 overlapping pairs of 66**. The widest silhouette is
  **0.064 × 0.041 m** inside a **0.075 × 0.065 m** cell. Repeat-lock ownership and the flight handoff
  remain device checks (the flight is P2.5's payoff, timed at 1.04 s here).
- [x] Empty slot visible from launch — `ShelfCheck` reads the markers' property blocks, not just
  their active flags: with nothing unlocked, **1 visible marker, dim**; with three unlocked,
  **4 visible (3 filled, 1 dim)** at the next slot. Nothing beyond the next slot is drawn.
- [x] Budget still met with 12 objects present — static estimate **and** built objects: **26/120** draw
  calls, **864/150000** worst-case triangles, **2** unique object/marker materials. Frame time remains
  a device capture.

any failure. It builds the shelf through the same `ShelfAnchor` fields `PersistenceBuilder` sets, and
the objects from the same prefab the lock payoff adopts.

This closes the *desk* half of all three boxes. It does not measure frame time, idle CPU, or how the
objects look in a headset.

## Owed to a device
- Idle CPU % for the breathing at 12 objects. **<1% or the idle is dropped**; the switch exists and is
  the whole fallback.
- Frame time with 12 objects and 12 markers in view. 26 draw calls is counted from built renderers, not
  measured on a GPU. A test rig must populate twelve distinct slots.
- Repeat-lock behaviour and the flight-to-slot handoff in a real session.
