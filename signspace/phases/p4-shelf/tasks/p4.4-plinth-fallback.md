# P4.4 — The plinth: never show an error, never depend on a surface

**Phase:** P4 · **Est:** 1 h · **Depends on:** P4.1 · **Workstream:** W2
**Gate fed:** G4

## Why this exists
Many judges will test sitting on a sofa, on a bed, or on a plane tray. A product that requires a desk fails the airplane-seat test for reasons that have nothing to do with the idea.

## Do this
1. [x] Build a virtual plinth that reads as a physical object: it has a shadow-catching gradient, a slight tilt, and it sits at the same visual height as a real surface would.
2. [x] Route to it silently when MRUK finds nothing, with no message.
3. [x] If a surface appears mid-session, offer a single quiet affordance to move to it — never auto-migrate.
4. [ ] Test in a room with the lights off and nothing detectable.

## Done when
- [ ] Plinth appears instantly when no surface exists *(startup waits for MRUK before calling `UsePlinth`; inspect/fix and test)*
- [ ] No error string, no spinner, no timeout *(structure check alone does not prove startup behavior; no Canvas, no error copy in the
  hierarchy; a headset screenshot is still owed)*
- [ ] Migration is user-initiated only *(a saved-home polling path can call `ResolveForCurrentRoom` automatically)*

## What was built

- `Plinth.prefab` has four parts and is generated by `Editor/PlinthBuilder.cs`: a top with a generated
  front-to-back gradient (`Materials/PlinthGradient.png`), a darker lip so it has thickness, a soft
  transparent shadow (`Materials/PlinthShadow.png`, queue 3000, ZWrite off) and a thin seam that
  catches light. Its local Y=0 is the top surface; it is instantiated 1 cm under the plate so the
  plate sits on it with no gap. It is tilted 4° — a perfectly level slab reads as UI.
- `UsePlinth` was already silent (no message, no spinner, no timeout). The check now proves it: the
  fallback leaves no Canvas and no error-looking text in the shelf hierarchy, and it happens in the
  same call rather than after a wait.
- `SurfaceOffer` is a small bead on the plate's right edge, hidden until `ShelfAnchor` raises
  `HasPendingSurfaceOffer`. `PollSurfaceOffer` only offers when there is **no saved home**, and the
  bead is the only caller of `AcceptSurfaceOffer`. Nothing migrates on its own.
- Fixed while checking: accepting an offer after a plinth fallback left `Result == Plinth`; a
  successful placement now reports `PlacedFresh`.

## Traps
- Auto-migrating when a surface appears — it teleports the user's progress in front of them

## Audit finding

The previous editor check calls `UsePlinth` directly; it does not exercise the real startup coroutine,
which waits for room readiness or a timeout before selecting the fallback. `PollNow` also retries a
saved anchor automatically. Both conflict with the stated first-frame and no-auto-migrate criteria.
They need an integrated policy fix and Unity/headset verification; no device behavior is asserted.
