# P4.6 — Shelf reflow for narrow field of view

**Phase:** P4 · **Est:** 1 h · **Depends on:** P4.1 · **Workstream:** W2
**Gate fed:** G4

## Why this exists
FoV-awareness is a named rubric technology, and the VR Glasses profile is 70°×66° against Quest's 110°×96°. A shelf designed for a wide cone simply disappears on the narrow one.

## Do this
1. Measure the current shelf extents in the XR Simulator at the VR Glasses profile.
2. Implement a reflow: beyond ~50° of horizontal offset, the shelf folds into a two-row tray closer to the body.
3. Confirm every object remains selectable and readable in both profiles.
4. Draw the safe cone (~55°) in the simulator for the video, then remove the debug draw.

## Done when
- [ ] All 12 slots legible at 70°×66°
- [ ] Reflow transition is smooth, not a pop
- [ ] Safe-cone diagram captured for the video

- Scaling the shelf down instead of reflowing it — scaling shrinks the tap targets below usable size
