# P5 — Content & capture

**Day 5–7 (Wed 11 – Fri 13 Nov) · Budget 12 h · Gate:** G5 — Is every shipped handshape traced to a named, credited Deaf signer — and does the product describe itself as practice?
**Status:** 🟡 5 of 7 complete as work (P5.2, P5.4, P5.5, P5.6, P5.7); P5.1 and P5.3 blocked on the signer — G5 open

## Objective
Get legitimate content into the build: real handshapes, from real signers, credited in the product, and a set chosen by measurement rather than ambition.

## Why it sits here
The legitimacy phase, and the mitigation for the only fatal risk in the project. It also has the longest lead time of anything here, which is why the pipeline opened on Day 1.

**Budget:** 12 h across three days, overlapping P4 and P6. The session itself is a fixed 90-minute appointment — everything else flexes around it.

## Inputs required before starting
- A booked signer with signed consent (from P0.9)
- The frozen pose format (P2.1)
- The trackability probe (P1.5)
- A capture rig

## Outputs — the phase is done when all of these are true
- [x] Twelve (or six) validated poses *(12: 6 shipped + 6 deferred, validated)*
- [x] Signer credits in-app *(object + prefab + check; wording approval owed)*
- [x] A final content decision documented *(`final-set-decision.md`)*
- [x] Blind-spot copy *(shipped; signer rewrite owed)*
- [ ] Signed consent, compensation and credit wording agreed *— blocked on the signer (P5.1/P5.3)*

## Gate
[`gate.md`](gate.md) — Is every shipped handshape traced to a named, credited Deaf signer — and does the product describe itself as practice?

## Traps specific to this phase
- Compressing the session to save an hour and losing half the takes
- Paraphrasing the signer instead of quoting them
- Choosing the final set before the data is in
