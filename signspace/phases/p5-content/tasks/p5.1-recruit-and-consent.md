# P5.1 — Recruit the signer and get consent signed

**Phase:** P5 · **Est:** 2 h · **Depends on:** P0.9 · **Workstream:** W3
**Gate fed:** G5
**Status:** 🟡 Artefacts prepared; human steps (send, book, sign, agree) remain

## Why this exists
**If this fails, the concept is replaced.** A hearing team shipping a sign-language product without Deaf participation is the one version of this idea that fails badly — and a Deaf judge will know within a minute.

## Do this
1. Confirm the session booking (target: Day 5) from the Day-1 outreach.
2. Send the brief in advance so the signer knows exactly what will be asked.
3. Sign consent covering: competition submission, the demo video, commercial release, and the right to name them — with an opt-out to be credited anonymously if preferred.
4. Agree compensation up front, in writing. Not exposure.
5. Agree the credit line wording together, before the session.

## Done when
- [ ] Consent signed and stored
- [ ] Compensation agreed in writing
- [ ] Credit wording agreed by the signer
