## Why this exists
The set must be chosen by evidence, not by ambition. The product is allowed to shrink; it is not allowed to include a shape that fails.

3. Recheck that the near-miss pairs survive the cut — if the cut breaks a pair, note which demo is lost.
4. If fewer than 8 shapes survive, fall back to 6 shapes / 1 family and say so in the description.
5. Update `SIGNSET.md` §2–5 and the near-miss pair table to match reality.
