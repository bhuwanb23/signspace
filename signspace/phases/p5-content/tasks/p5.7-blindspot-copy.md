# P5.7 — Write the blind-spot copy with the signer

**Phase:** P5 · **Est:** 1 h · **Depends on:** P5.3 · **Workstream:** W3
**Gate fed:** G5
**Status:** 🟡 copy drafted and shipped in the panel; the signer's rewrite is owed

## Why this exists
Explaining a platform limitation in front of a Deaf audience is delicate. The words must be right, and the signer is the person to get them right.

## Do this
1. Draft the panel copy: what the exclusion is, why it happens, and what changes it.
2. Have the signer read it and rewrite anything that sounds like an excuse or a simplification.
3. Keep the tone factual: *the cameras cannot see a finger behind a finger.*
4. Include the forward-looking line about future hardware, without promising a date.

## Done when
- [ ] Copy approved by the signer *(blocked: P5.3)*
- [x] Factual, no apology, no over-promise *(one mechanism sentence per shape; forward line has no date)*
- [x] One sentence per excluded shape maximum *(M, N, R, T — from `trackability.json` `note`)*
