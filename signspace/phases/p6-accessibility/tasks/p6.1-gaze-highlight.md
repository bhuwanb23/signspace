# P6.1 — Eye-gaze interaction on the finger-highlight layer

**Phase:** P6 · **Est:** 2 h · **Depends on:** P2.2 · **Workstream:** W2
**Gate fed:** G6

## Why this exists
You cannot point at a finger while both hands are occupied holding and matching. Gaze is not a gimmick here — it is the only input available at that moment.

## Do this
1. Add the ISDK Eye Gaze Interaction building block to the rig.
2. Make each finger a gaze target with a generous hit volume (a capsule, not a plane).
3. On gaze, highlight the finger and show its position label (from the signer's own description where possible).
4. Add a dwell timer (start 700 ms) as the confirmation path, with pinch as the alternative.
5. Test at 0.4 m and 0.6 m: fingers are ~15 mm apart, which is the real precision question.

## Done when
- [ ] Highlight follows gaze accurately at both distances — **NOT RUN** (needs a headset; HUMAN-TASKS H14)
- [x] Dwell and pinch both confirm
- [x] No accidental highlights when looking around — only fingertips and objects with a `DwellAction` produce a label; the 5-minute quiet session is still **NOT RUN**
